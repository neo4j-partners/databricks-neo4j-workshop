= Part 1: Graph Foundations

Part 1 gets you a graph database of your own and puts the fleet in it. Everything after this reads what these two labs write.

.**At a Glance**
* **You end with an AuraDB Free instance** holding the full Aircraft Digital Twin: Aircraft, System, Component, Sensor, Flight, Delay, MaintenanceEvent and Airport.
* **Your Neo4j password lands in a Databricks secret scope** in Lab 1, and every later lab reads it from there rather than asking again.
* **The Spark Connector is the bridge.** Lab 2 writes from a Databricks DataFrame straight into the graph, which is the same path a production pipeline uses.
* **About 65 minutes** across the two labs.
