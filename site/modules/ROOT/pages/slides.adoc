= Slide Decks

Every deck is embedded on its own page. Each page also carries an **Open full screen** link, which is the better way to present or to read one on a small screen.

.**At a Glance**
* **Eight workshop decks**, in the order an instructor presents them.
* **Seven background decks**, deeper material that no lab depends on.
* **Decks are built, not committed.** `slides/build-slides.sh` renders the Markdown sources into HTML, and CI runs it before every publish.
